Problems Identified

1. Date Parser (parser) · 379 reports

The shared date parser reads dates month-first (MM/DD), while tenants in day-first locales write day-first (DD/MM).

  • Reports covered: 379 reports.
  • Channels: app, email, and form.
  • Tenants: 11 tenants in day-first locales (de-DE, es-ES, en-GB, nl-NL, cs-CZ, it-IT, fr-FR, pl-PL, pt-PT, da-DK, en-IE).
  • Desk note: 5 reports were initially read as one-day moves, but belong to this cause.

2. Booking-v3 Date Hint (hint) · 33 reports

Tenants using the booking-v3 form display an MM/DD/YYYY placeholder hint. Users in day-first locales enter dates month-first following the hint, but the form interprets their input using the tenant's day-first locale (DD/MM/YYYY).

  • Reports covered: 33 reports.
  • Channels: form only.
  • Tenants: 3 tenants running booking-v3 in day-first locales (alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour).
